# RJS Cottage — Research notes

Research date: **1 November 2026**. Prepared for the Option 01 website exploration.

The point of this file is to keep **researched facts** separate from **design assumptions**.
Each fact carries its source. Confidence: **High** = first-party or operator source that agrees with
the client's own media. **Medium** = one operator source only. **Low** = third-party aggregator,
conflicting or unverified.

## 1. What RJS Cottage is

- RJS Cottage is a set of **three private-pool cottages** (RJS Cottage 1, 2 and 3) in Cisarua, Puncak,
  Kabupaten Bogor. Each unit is rented whole, with its own pool. *(High: therainvillas.com unit pages,
  Instagram highlights "RJS COTTAGE 1/2/3", client video)*
- The units are also listed and sold by **The Rain Villas** ("Private Collection"), an operator/agency
  with 30+ private-pool villas in Cisarua. On therainvillas.com they appear as "Rjs Cottage 1/2/3", with
  pages, prices, rules and a booking flow. *(High: therainvillas.com)*
  - One guest Story in the REVIEW highlight tags both `@therainvillas` and `@rjscottage`.
  - Two client videos carry a small The Rain Villas watermark.
  - **We do not know the commercial relationship** (owner vs. operator vs. reseller). See §9.
- Positioning on the operator site (their copy):
  - Unit 1: "Cottage cozy dengan private pool, gazebo, dan halaman luas. Cocok untuk family getaway yang santai."
  - Units 2 and 3: "Cottage nyaman dengan private pool dan chill space."

## 4. Booking flow & policies

Source: therainvillas.com unit FAQ and /syarat-ketentuan. **These are the operator's rules. The owner must confirm them for RJS-direct bookings.**

1. Contact the admin on WhatsApp.
2. Fill in the booking form.
3. Pay a deposit (**DP**) of at least **30%**. The DP is **non-refundable**.
4. Pay the balance by **H-3** (3 days before check-in). An unpaid balance forfeits the DP.
- Reschedule allowed up to **H-7**, with an additional DP.
- **Check-out 12.00.** Late check-out costs Rp 500.000. **Check-in:** "sesuai kesepakatan" (operator). Casai says 14:00 (Low).
- Over capacity: **Rp 50.000 per extra guest**.
- **Quiet hours until 22.00.** No **pets**, no **alcohol**, no drugs or sharp weapons. Guests pay for damage.
- Availability calendar exists on the operator site (therainvillas.com/availability). There is no real-time engine on any RJS-owned channel.

## 8. Surroundings (general knowledge, not RJS-specific)

- Route: Jakarta → Tol Jagorawi → exit Ciawi/Gadog → Jl. Raya Puncak → Cisarua. Tugu Utara is a Puncak
  village known for tea plantations and its tourism-village programme (RRI, "Desa Wisata Tugu Utara").
- Weekend/holiday traffic in Puncak is managed with **one-way (sistem satu arah)** windows and odd–even
  rules. Guests should check the schedule before leaving. *(Public knowledge; exact schedules vary, so the
  site advises checking rather than quoting times.)*
- Distances to attractions were listed only by the Casai feed (from the conflicting Cilama address), so they **aren't used**.
